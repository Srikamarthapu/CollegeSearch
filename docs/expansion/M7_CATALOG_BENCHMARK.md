# M7 catalog coverage benchmark — October 4, 2026

## Recommendation

Use the complete **3,912-UNITID** roster supportable from the pinned U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard institution file. This is the largest defensible roster for the current source and product scope: every listed UNITID is federally marked operating in the April 30, 2026 PEPS snapshot, serves undergraduate levels according to the file's classification, is a 2-year or 4-year institution, and belongs to a U.S. state, DC, or a U.S. territory. Include all three ownership classes and retain branch UNITIDs separately. Describe the result as a federal undergraduate-serving institution roster as of the snapshot date; it is not a guarantee that a school is operating today or a census of every postsecondary provider.

The selected roster includes 2,486 four-year-level and 1,426 two-year-level institutions; 1,666 public, 1,486 private nonprofit, and 760 private for-profit institutions; 3,430 `MAIN=1` and 482 `MAIN=0` UNITIDs; and 998 certificate-predominant, 970 associate-predominant, and 1,944 bachelor's-predominant institutions. It covers the 50 states and DC (3,831 records), plus Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands (81 records). These classifications are not mutually exclusive descriptions of every credential offered: `PREDDEG` means predominant award category, while `ICLEVEL` means the highest level offered. The selected `PREDDEG=1` group is certificate-predominant, so “undergraduate-serving” is more accurate than “bachelor's degree-granting.”

## Scope and evidence rules

The reproducible selection is over **all** UNITIDs in the June 10, 2026 `Most-Recent-Cohorts-Institution_06102026.zip` artifact whose fields satisfy:

- `STABBR` is one of the 50 states, DC, PR, GU, VI, AS, or MP;
- `CURROPER=1`, which the Scorecard dictionary identifies as PEPS status as of April 30, 2026;
- `ICLEVEL` is 1 (four-year) or 2 (two-year);
- `PREDDEG` is 1, 2, or 3 (certificate-, associate-, or bachelor's-predominant); and
- `CONTROL` is 1, 2, or 3 (public, private nonprofit, or private for-profit).

`MAIN=0` branch UNITIDs are included as distinct profiles. This does not claim that every federal outcome is campus-specific: College Scorecard documentation says some parent institutions report combined data for multiple IPEDS units. Retain each UNITID's exact facts and locators, and disclose the source's aggregation where relevant. The pinned file excludes solely administrative offices and generally covers Title IV institutions that report to IPEDS, plus some non-Title-IV institutions meeting similar criteria; it is not a universal directory of every training provider. Freely Associated States are outside this report's U.S.-territory definition and selection.

The local ZIP SHA-256 is `f56a181b000ca4914e924c16b6b81dcc656e25aeb2ac68ab7d271ac0f29ffd58`. The current official Scorecard [data page](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) links the downloads and documentation. The [institution-level technical documentation](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/files/InstitutionDataDocumentation.pdf) describes the source universe and notes that some records share outcomes across parent/child IPEDS units. The [official data dictionary](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/files/CollegeScorecardDataDictionary.xlsx) provides the cohort map: `CURROPER` is PEPS as of 2026-04-30; `ICLEVEL` and `CONTROL` describe academic year 2025-26; `PREDDEG` describes AY 2024-25 as reported in IPEDS DCY 2025-26. These dates differ, so the roster should always carry the source snapshot date and each measure's own period.

The exact local filter is recorded in `data/college-catalog.json`. Independent validation uses `scripts/audit-catalog-federal-evidence.mjs` against the pinned ZIP; that audit compares exact source identities, all direct federal values, selected-roster membership, and both associate/bachelor broad-field evidence. On the final 38-field release, it passes with 3,912 of 3,912 identities matched, 31,296 direct observation values checked (26,615 numeric and 4,681 unavailable), 47,175 broad-field rows checked, and 3,890 federal graduation-cohort checks. Twenty-two graduation observations are approved institution-source overlays and were not compared to the federal file. The audit preserved nine negative average-net-price observations; those values must retain their context rather than be clamped. Missing and suppressed fields remain missing. Reproduce with `node scripts/audit-catalog-federal-evidence.mjs --report work/m7-federal-audit-final38.json` after obtaining the exact ZIP named in the manifest.

## Program-field coverage

The current importer supports all **38** `PCIPxx` broad fields in the official institution file and the matching 76 `CIPxxBACHL`/`CIPxxASSOC` program-availability columns. Applying the rule “affirmative bachelor's or associate availability code plus a numeric award share” yields 47,175 college-field records across the pinned cohort; 3,920 records have both degree levels available. The displayed field names are broad award-share categories, not specific majors. The supported field families span agriculture and natural resources, architecture, cultural and area studies, communications, computing, personal and culinary services, education, engineering and technologies, languages, family sciences, legal studies, humanities, library science, biological sciences, math, military technologies, interdisciplinary studies, recreation, philosophy and religion, physical and science technologies, psychology, public safety, public administration, social sciences, skilled trades, transportation, arts, health, business, and history. The official-name map is in `app/lib/broad-fields.ts`; the field-by-field audit counts are in the ignored `work/m7-cip-fields.json` generated from the pinned official dictionary and ZIP.

Each field row keeps its exact `PCIP` share and affirmative bachelor/associate `CIP` source columns. A zero share is retained when a qualifying availability code exists; it means zero share of reported institution-wide awards in this data, not absence of a program. A positive `PCIP` share is not a program-specific admission rate, and no `PCIP` value establishes a particular concentration. Preserve sparse categories as sparse; for example, the pinned source has only 45 Library Science and 61 Military Technologies rows. Do not add a field-specific claim when the source has no affirmative availability code or numeric share.

For the knowledge index, the final compiler stores all three exact facts per broad field and groups only the descriptive availability text into same-source, same-cohort passages capped at 1,900 characters. This reduced 51,087 ungrouped passages (one identity and one field passage per college-field row) to 9,070 deterministic passages (3,912 identity and 5,158 program chunks) for this release while keeping all 173,029 structured facts. Passage locators list the precise `CIP` availability columns in that chunk; each recommendation's structured field citation still carries that institution's exact `PCIP` plus available `CIP` columns. A citation renderer must use that field-level record when making a specific program-availability claim rather than showing a chunk's full locator list as though it named only one field.

## Operational limits and review notes

`CURROPER=1` is a dated federal/PEPS status, not live verification, accreditation status, or a promise that an institution is accepting applicants. Each new Scorecard release should regenerate the roster from the documented filter, compare UNITID and slug identities against the published release, and require explicit review before removing a previously published saved ID. Preserve old UNITIDs and slugs. Re-run the full audit before publishing refreshed data.

Program and institution statistics have different periods: the cohort map says the 2-year completion measure `C150_L4` is a Fall 2021 or AY 2021-22 cohort, while the 4-year `C150_4` field uses a Fall 2018 or AY 2018-19 cohort. Label those separately. The current-year operating flag cannot make older admissions, price, earnings, or program data current; every displayed fact needs its own source field, cohort, and period.
